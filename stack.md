## Books

<section class="content-grid animate-in">
  <article class="page-card">
    <h3><a href="https://amzn.to/40orUvs" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AI Engineering</a></h3>
    <p><strong>O'Reilly · 2025</strong></p>
    <p>Practical reference for building applications on top of foundation models — model selection, evaluation, deployment, and product patterns.</p>
    <div class="tech-tags"><span class="tech-tag accent">AI/ML</span><span class="tech-tag">Systems</span></div>
  </article>
  <article class="page-card">
    <h3><a href="https://www.amazon.com/Designing-Machine-Learning-Systems-Production-Ready/dp/1098107969" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Designing Machine Learning Systems</a></h3>
    <p><strong>O'Reilly · 2022</strong></p>
    <p>System-design view of ML in production: data pipelines, features, training loops, model monitoring, reliability, and iteration under real constraints.</p>
    <div class="tech-tags"><span class="tech-tag accent">ML Systems</span><span class="tech-tag">Data Engineering</span></div>
  </article>
  <article class="page-card">
    <h3><a href="https://huyenchip.com/ml-interviews-book/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Machine Learning Interviews</a></h3>
    <p><strong>Chip Huyen · 2021</strong></p>
    <p>Interview preparation covering ML fundamentals, system design, practical judgment, and hiring-process expectations for ML engineering roles.</p>
    <div class="tech-tags"><span class="tech-tag purple">Interview Prep</span><span class="tech-tag">ML</span></div>
  </article>
</section>
